FAQ

How is CGPA different from GPA?

GPA covers one semester. CGPA is the credit-weighted average across all semesters, so enter your previous CGPA and total credits above to include them.

Is this the official Easwari Engineering College calculator?

No. This is an unofficial student project. Always confirm your final GPA and CGPA with your official results and the college.
